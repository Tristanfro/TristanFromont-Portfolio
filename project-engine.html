Inline-4 Engine Crankshaft Assembly

SolidWorks CAD Design Kinematics Assembly
1/4

1. Project Overview & Objectives

This project focuses on the engineering design, kinematic calculation, and volumetric modeling of a complete 4-cylinder in-line engine assembly. The primary objective was to achieve optimal dynamic balancing of rotating masses while respecting real-world manufacturing constraints.

2. Methodology & Design Constraints

The modeling process was conducted under SolidWorks using a fully parametric CAD approach. Key engineering specifications included:

  • Bore & Stroke Sizing: Standard stroke ratio calculated for targeted engine displacement.
  • Mass Balancing: Web counterweight design calculated to compensate for primary and secondary inertia forces.
  • Material Selection: Forged steel properties applied for stress analysis compatibility.
"Precision in journal alignment and mass distribution directly impacts the overall harmonic stability at high RPM."

3. Key Results & Achievements

The final assembly achieved smooth rotational clearance, proper volumetric piston placement, and minimized mass unbalance. Detailed engineering drawings and exploded views were exported for manufacturing analysis.

  • Fully dynamic assembly constraint check passed without collisions.
  • Optimized counterweight volume reduction by 8% without compromising balancing.
